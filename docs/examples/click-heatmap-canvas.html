Click density heatmap for session viewport

Recorded click events from inlined sessionData are aggregated into a binned heatmap on a canvas sized to metadata.viewport. Brighter / warmer colors mean more clicks landed in that region of the viewport (after binning and light smoothing). This page does not fetch session payloads over the network; data is synthetic and stable for reviewers and automation. For focus and keyboard expectations when you fork this pattern, see docs/a11y/keyboard-model.md.
